SportHub Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026
Application ID: com.solutionwin.app

This Privacy Policy explains how the SportHub Android application (the “App”) handles information. By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The App allows you to create notes, schedule matches and training sessions, record stopwatch laps, and save appearance preferences. This information is stored locally on your device. We do not receive this locally stored content. You can remove it through the App where available, by clearing the App’s data, or by uninstalling the App.

2. Information processed when you use connected features

The App may contact Google Firebase to retrieve configuration required for operation. During these requests, Google may process standard technical information such as your IP address, device information, network information, and request timestamps. The App does not use Firebase to upload your notes or calendar entries.

When you open a third-party news source, media service, or other online content, that provider may collect information under its own privacy policy and may use cookies or similar technologies. We do not control third-party services.

3. QR codes

The QR scanner uses a camera-based scanning service provided through Google Play services. Scanned content is shown to you first. A detected link is opened only after you choose to open it. You should verify destinations before continuing.

4. Device permissions

Depending on the feature you use, the App may request access to:

  • Camera for QR scanning, photo capture, or other camera-enabled features.
  • Microphone when an audio-enabled feature requires it.
  • Location when a location-enabled feature requires it.
  • Notifications for match and training reminders.
  • Files and media when you choose a file, attach a screenshot, or download content.

Permissions are requested contextually. You can deny or revoke them at any time in Android settings, although the related feature may then be unavailable.

5. Feedback and attachments

If you choose to send feedback, the App opens a compatible sharing or email application selected by you. Your message, optional contact details, and any attachment are transmitted only after you confirm the action. The receiving application and service process that information under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Downloads and clipboard

Files are downloaded only after an action initiated through the App and are managed by Android’s download system. Clipboard access is used only when a feature you interact with requests copying or pasting content. Clipboard information is not sent to us.

7. Third-party services

The App may use services supplied by Google, including Firebase and Google Play services. Their handling of information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. External links and content providers may have separate policies that you should review.

8. Data sales and advertising

We do not sell your personal information. The current version of the App does not provide user accounts and does not use locally created notes or calendar entries for advertising.

9. Data retention

Locally stored App data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App’s storage, or uninstall the App. Information voluntarily sent through an external feedback channel is retained according to that channel’s policies and as reasonably necessary to respond to your request.

10. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a feedback channel, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

11. Security

We use reasonable measures to limit unnecessary data processing and rely on Android security controls for local storage and permissions. No method of storage or electronic transmission is completely secure.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or applicable requirements change. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Solution Win through the project’s GitHub issue page.
